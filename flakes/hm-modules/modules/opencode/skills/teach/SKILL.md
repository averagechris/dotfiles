# Teach

Apply `impactful-writing`. Start with the smallest useful mental model and add depth at the person's pace.

- Tie the explanation to their question and what they already know.
- Show the path from trigger to effect. Use concrete examples or observations.
- Explain why the design works and where its limits are.
- Use a diagram when the relationships are easier to see than describe.

Stop once the current layer is clear. Let the person choose where to go deeper.
