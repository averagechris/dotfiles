# Granola meeting notes

Use Granola when a meeting may contain the rationale. Load the `granola-meeting-context` skill and use its workflow or the `granola` CLI when available. Keep searches bounded by feature terms, participant names, project names, and a date range around the change.

Meeting notes may directly record a decision and its stated reason. They do not prove final approval or implementation. Corroborate decisions against Linear, repository Markdown, PRs, or shipped code.

Record the meeting title, date, attendees when available, note or transcript section, and a stable ID or link. Distinguish a transcript quote from a generated or edited summary. Transcripts can misidentify speakers or words, while summaries can omit disagreement. Note missing dates, partial sync, access limits, and searches with no results.

Quote only the text needed to support the claim. Redact personal data, customer details, credentials, and sensitive discussion as `[REDACTED]` without hiding the decision context.
