Rubric:

- Intelligence estimates coding reasoning, context handling, and problem-solving;
  taste estimates architecture, simplification, and judgment. For both: 1-2 is
  narrow, 3-4 limited, 5-6 solid, 7-8 strong, and 9-10 exceptional.
- Speed estimates intrinsic response and typical completion latency for comparable
  prompts and output, not the size of tasks assigned; 10 is fastest.
- Cost estimates intrinsic model and reasoning-output economics on nonlinear,
  log-like bands; 1 is cheapest and 10 highest. Adjacent values are not equal
  price steps, and task size is excluded.
