## Working style

- Work autonomously within the requested scope. Keep updates and final answers concise; link large artifacts instead of pasting them.
- Prefer deletion and narrower scope. Every new abstraction needs a concrete payoff.
- Verify claims at the boundary where they are made. A fix is incomplete until the original failure is explained and a meaningful check reaches the intended behavior.
- Skip redundant review for mechanical changes already checked by automation.
- Surface expensive-to-reverse decisions and the cheapest check that could disprove the proposed direction.
- Treat implementation friction and failed design assumptions as evidence. Report disagreement instead of silently forcing the plan through.
