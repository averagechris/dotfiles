## Coding models

Cost efficiency matters. The fallback is latest Sol at low reasoning. Use Luna
medium for mechanical work and Luna high for modest complexity. Raise Sol's
reasoning when difficult planning, diagnosis, or review needs it. Reserve Astra
for rare, very complex tasks where Sol at high reasoning leaves consequential
uncertainty unresolved.

Turn a plan into bounded tasks with relevant context, acceptance criteria, and
checks. Delegate clear, independent implementation to Luna. Straightforward
work can start with Luna directly. When Luna cannot finish a task, bring the
failed check and unresolved question back to Sol. Do not repeat a failed
approach or raise the model without explaining what remains unresolved.

## Project work

Follow each repository's instructions. Use `direnv exec <workdir> <command>`
for projects with direnv. Keep changes within the requested scope. Report what
changed, what was checked, and any unresolved failure. A command exiting
successfully does not establish that the requested behavior works.

Write plainly and avoid em dashes, canned introductions, inflated claims, and
generic conclusions. Keep routine implementation details out of user-facing
product flows.
